## master

Create videos that feel like premium claymation documentary explainers. Every visual should render as tactile, handcrafted clay stop-motion — rounded, slightly imperfect geometry with visible fingerprint-like surface quality. Characters are stylized clay mannequins with smooth matte finishes and soft rounded joints. Environments are miniature diorama-style sets with visible clay walls, dirt floors, and scaled-down props. Color palette is warm, saturated earthtones with pops of vivid accent color. Lighting is soft and diffused like a lightbox over a stop-motion set — no harsh shadows, gentle ambient occlusion only.

SHOT PLANNING GUIDANCE FOR THIS AESTHETIC:

- Diorama environments can be orbited freely — freeze_orbit is the primary spatial orientation tool, use it for architectural reveals, model introductions, and layout explanations
- Alternate wide (scale) and close/extreme-close (texture) shots deliberately to create the intimacy-scale rhythm of authentic stop-motion filmmaking
- All MG elements must feel like PROPS inside the clay world, not external digital overlays: clay-stamped lettering, torn clay-paper, masking tape, cork board evidence walls, clay signpost map labels, clay-strip timeline boards
- Character dossier cards use clay-sketch portrait style with clay-stamp status labels that hit the card with physical kinetic energy
- Slap annotations look like torn clay-paper or masking tape physically entering frame from off-screen
- Territory maps use clay-surface backgrounds with colored putty spreading organically across them
- Ghost figures show fingerprint clay texture visible through the translucent silhouette body
